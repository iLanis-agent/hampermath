Hamper Math

The 370 mangoes at 9 mangoes a hamper take 42 hampers, the last hamper holding 1 mango.

Boundary: arithmetic only Hamper Math counts an event's festival mangoes with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the hampers the mangoes take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a mango, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your mangoes, your hampers or your venue and does not know whether the mangoes will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a hamper is worth its price or a mango is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 370 mangoes, 9 mangoes a hamper, 26 mangoes a week, $8.75 a hamper and 6 mangoes a minute.

The weeks truth

The 370 mangoes at 26 mangoes a week last 14 full weeks with 6 mangoes left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 42 hampers at $8.75 a hamper come to $367.50, $0.99 a mango.

The time truth

The 370 mangoes at 6 mangoes a minute take 62 minutes, 1 hour 2 minutes.
