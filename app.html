Hamper Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's festival mangoes with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the hampers the mangoes take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a mango, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. The mangoes, the hamper size, the weekly use, the hamper price and the packing speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your mangoes, your hampers or your venue and does not know whether the mangoes will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a hamper is worth its price or a mango is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The hampers truth

Hampers: the mangoes divided by the mangoes a hamper, rounded up, with what the last hamper holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the mangoes divided by the mangoes used a week, with the mangoes left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the hampers times the hamper price, to the cent, and spread over the mangoes.

The time truth

Time: the mangoes divided by the mangoes packed a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Hamper Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
